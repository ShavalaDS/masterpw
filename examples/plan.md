# Research plan — Redesigning the commute

One page. Questions, methods, who, when. This plan is the container everything else answers to: every note collected from here on links back to it.

## The brief

Brief 1 — Redesigning the Commute: design a feature that improves one specific moment of the daily commute within an existing transport service. Candidate moments: waiting at the station, planning a trip, recovering from a delay, choosing between transport modes, the first/last mile.

## Research questions

1. Which moment of the commute causes the most friction for daily rail commuters in Milan — and when exactly does it happen?
2. How do commuters get delay information today, and where does that chain break down?
3. What do commuters actually do while waiting, and what would make that time feel shorter?
4. What tools and services do they already use, and where is the information scattered?

## Human-led activities — 3 interviews

Semi-structured, ~25 minutes each, at Milano Centrale or immediately after the commute, consent recorded at the start of the recording.

| # | Who | Commute | Focus | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marta Bianchi, 34 — product manager | Monza → Milano Centrale by regional train, 5 days/week | Delays, information checking, recalculating | 2026-10-08, 07:55 |
| 2 | Alessandro Ferri, 41 — consultant | Bergamo → Milano Centrale by regional train, 3 days/week | Long rides, crowding, waiting time, what he does while waiting | 2026-10-09, 08:10 |
| 3 | Sofia Colombo, 27 — retail shift worker | Lodi → Milano Centrale, off-peak and weekend shifts | Off-peak commuting, apps, planning the night before | 2026-10-10, 16:30 |

Interview guide (same for all three, follow unexpected threads): current commute routine → the last time something went wrong → how they found out → what they did → what they wished existed. Consent line read before recording.

## AI-led activity — desk research on commuting trends

Topic: how long Europeans spend commuting, and how they feel about it — the macro context for a Milan-focused feature.

- Euronews / SD Worx survey (2026) — European average commute times by country
- Eurostat, EU Labour Force Survey — commuting time distribution and averages
- European Court of Auditors, Special Report SR-2026-05 — to verify before use

Done during the Lesson 02 hands-on → `desk.md`.

## Success criteria

From the brief: the feature integrates into an existing service, demonstrably reduces friction or anxiety, uses AI meaningfully — not as a gimmick — and can be prototyped within the course. Evidence collected here must be enough to choose *one* moment and defend the choice.
