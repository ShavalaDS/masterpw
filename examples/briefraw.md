Redesigning grocery delivery.

The challenge.

Design a feature that improves a specific moment of the online grocery delivery experience within an existing supermarket chain's service — e.g., Esselunga, Coop, Carrefour. Not restaurant food delivery: the supermarket basket, ordered online and delivered home.
Why it matters.

The weekly grocery shop is one of the most repetitive household routines: the basket is big, weekly and perishable. Small improvements compound into significant time, cost and food-waste gains. AI offers new opportunities to personalize the basket, reduce uncertainty about substitutions and freshness, and make the weekly shop feel predictable.
Key pain points.

Items unavailable at picking time — substitutions chosen blindly
Rigid delivery windows and missed slots
Freshness and expiry concerns when shopping sight unseen
Rebuilding the same basket from scratch every week
Unclear quantities and pricing for weighted products
Information scattered between store app, delivery app and email receipts
Design focus.

Pick one specific moment or problem in the grocery delivery journey:
Building the weekly basket
Handling substitutions when items are unavailable
Choosing the delivery slot
Receiving the order at the door
Managing recurring purchases
Success criteria.

The feature integrates naturally into an existing supermarket service
It demonstrably reduces friction, effort or food waste
It uses AI in a meaningful way — not as a gimmick
It can be prototyped and tested within the course
Example directions.

An AI substitution chooser that learns your preferences and explains each swap
A smart weekly basket that rebuilds itself from your habits and household size
A freshness-aware delivery planner that sequences slots by expiry dates
A zero-waste meal planner that turns the basket into the week's dinners
Deliverables.

Research and insights · Concept and feature map · User flows and wireframes · High-fidelity screens · Working prototype or coded demo · Case study presentation.
